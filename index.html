GTA Vice Hours Uploader

An in-house publishing tool for one YouTube channel: GTA Vice Hours (@gtavicehours).

This tool uses YouTube API Services

What it does

GTA Vice Hours posts short, edited clips of streamers playing Grand Theft Auto VI. It works with streamers through clipping campaigns, which pay for views on the clips and require a credit and paid-promotion disclosure. The tool:

  • uploads the channel's own finished, edited Shorts to the channel, with their title and description, marked as paid promotion;
  • reads the view, like and comment counts of the channel's own videos, to see which clips work and to report views to the campaigns that sponsor them.

It runs on a single computer belonging to the channel's owner. It signs in only to the channel's own Google account. It is not offered to other users, and it does not access anyone else's channel or data.
