The tool

GTA Vice Hours Uploader is an in-house tool. It publishes edited clips to the GTA Vice Hours accounts on YouTube (@gtavicehours), TikTok (@gtavicehours) and Instagram (@gtavicehours), and reads those accounts' own video statistics. It is used only by the accounts' owner, and is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.

YouTube Terms of Service

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Anyone using the tool agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The tool follows the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

TikTok and Instagram terms

Posting to TikTok through the tool is subject to the TikTok Terms of Service, TikTok's Community Guidelines, its Branded Content Policy and Music Usage Confirmation. Each TikTok post is reviewed in the tool by the owner, who chooses its privacy setting and interaction settings and confirms it before it is posted. Posting to Instagram is subject to the Instagram Terms of Use.

Content rules the tool follows

  • Every clip is materially edited: captions, layout, on-screen hook and credit. Clips are published with the streamer's credit, as their clipping campaign requires.
  • Every clip a campaign pays for is disclosed as paid promotion (on TikTok, as branded content).
  • No views, likes or subscribers are bought. No bots or automated engagement are used. Each video is uploaded once to each account.
  • No story spoilers, leaks or cutscene compilations are published.
