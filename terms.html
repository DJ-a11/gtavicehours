The tool

GTA Vice Hours Uploader is an in-house tool. It publishes edited clips to the YouTube channel GTA Vice Hours, and reads that channel's own video statistics. It is used only by the channel's owner, and is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else.

YouTube Terms of Service

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Anyone using the tool agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. The tool follows the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

Content rules the tool follows

  • Every clip is materially edited: captions, layout, on-screen hook and credit. Clips are published with the streamer's credit, as their clipping campaign requires.
  • Every clip is marked as paid promotion, because campaigns pay for views.
  • No views, likes or subscribers are bought. No bots or automated engagement are used. Each video is uploaded once, to one channel.
  • No story spoilers, leaks or cutscene compilations are published.
