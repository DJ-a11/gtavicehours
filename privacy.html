Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

GTA Vice Hours Uploader (the "tool") is an in-house tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel GTA Vice Hours, which also posts as @gtavicehours on TikTok and @gtavicehours on Instagram. It uses YouTube API Services, TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API, and Instagram's API. This policy explains what the tool accesses through those services, and what it does with it.

YouTube API Services and Google's Privacy Policy

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using the tool, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok and Instagram

Use of TikTok through the tool is also subject to the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy. Use of Instagram is subject to the Instagram Terms of Use and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Whose data

The tool has one user: the channel's owner. It signs in (OAuth 2.0) only to the GTA Vice Hours accounts on Google, TikTok and Instagram. It does not let anyone else sign in, and it does not collect data about viewers or other users of those services.

What the tool accesses

  • Uploading (scope youtube.upload): it uploads the channel's own edited videos, with their title, description and tags.
  • Reading (scope youtube.readonly): it reads public statistics (view, like and comment counts) of the channel's own videos, and the channel's subscriber and video counts.

On TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API):

  • Account basics (scope user.info.basic): the account's ID, display name and avatar, shown in the tool so the owner can see which account a post goes to.
  • Posting (scope video.publish): the account's posting options (privacy levels it allows, and whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed), and posting a video that the owner has reviewed, captioned and approved in the tool. Nothing is posted to TikTok without the owner's click.

On Instagram: the account's username, and publishing the account's own Reels with their captions.

It does not read comments, messages, followers, viewer identities, watch history or any other account's private data.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Video statistics are used only to judge which clips perform well, and to report views to the clipping campaigns that sponsor the clips. Those campaigns see the public video links and public view counts.
  • Statistics are stored in a file on the owner's own computer and refreshed at most once an hour. OAuth tokens for each service are stored on that computer only. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.
  • The tool uses no cookies or tracking, and serves no advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

Statistics held by the tool are replaced at each refresh. They are deleted within 30 days of the tool stopping use of YouTube API Services, or of access being revoked. Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. TikTok access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services permissions; Instagram access under Settings, Apps and websites. When access is revoked, the tool deletes its stored token and the statistics it holds.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page, with a new date. For questions or deletion requests, open an issue at github.com/DJ-a11/gtavicehours/issues.
